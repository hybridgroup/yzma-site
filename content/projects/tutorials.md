### Running local LLMs and VLMs on the Arduino UNO Q with yzma

Marc Pous ([@mpous](https://github.com/mpous)) shows how to run local LLMs and VLMs on the [Arduino UNO Q](https://docs.arduino.cc/hardware/uno-q/) with yzma and `llama.cpp`.

https://projecthub.arduino.cc/marc-edgeimpulse/running-local-llms-and-vlms-on-the-arduino-uno-q-with-yzma-74e288

https://github.com/mi-bear/yzma-handson
